# Changelog

Newest first. Versions follow semver on `pack.json` / `contract.json`:
**patch** = data corrections; **minor** = new layers or optional fields;
**major** = removed or renamed published fields/layers.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-23 (C28)

First versioned public contract: `version` on `pack.json`, `contract.json`,
`docs/consuming-the-pack.md`, and `scripts/check-contract.py`.

## 0.9.0 — 2026-09-23 (C27)

Snap guides (`guides.geojson`), pack-frame `grid.json` with worked example, and
`construction_grid` (`authority: symbolic`, `evidence: design-intent`).

## 0.8.0 — 2026-09-23 (C26)

`sky-events.json` for gathering bands and structures; `alignments.geojson`;
mourner/suncalc cross-check of flat sunrise/sunset.

## 0.7.0 — 2026-09-23 (C25)

`trees-instances.json` (16 oak archetypes, 3663 instances), `budget.json`,
hardened LODs via gltfpack.

## 0.6.0 — 2026-09-23 (C24)

`walkable.geojson`, `walk-graph.json`, `models/collision.glb`.

## 0.5.0 — 2026-09-22 (C23)

site-grounds outline corrected (floors ∪ +3 m); `licence-manifest.json` expanded.

## 0.4.0 — 2026-09-22 (C22)

Exclusive first-hit D8 water catchments; drawable trees/horizon/terrain hillshade
in `pack-layers.json`.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-09-22 (C21)

Drawable atlas (`pack-layers.json`); water re-sited; checks that compare two
independent quantities.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-09-22 (C20)

`buildable` vs `gathering` split; defensible-space rings; water-harvest sized to
25 mm.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-22 (C19)

Drawable land rasters (16-bit PNG + sidecar decode) and gathering overlay.
